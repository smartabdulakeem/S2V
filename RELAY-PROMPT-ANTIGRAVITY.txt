Paste this whole file, unchanged, into a FRESH Antigravity window at every handoff.

It never needs editing. RELAY-STATE.json names the current brief, so this prompt
stays constant for every slice of every milestone.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

Read ANTIGRAVITY-RULES.md, then RELAY-STATE.json.

Open the brief named in brief_file and execute it exactly.

Before changing anything, satisfy the verification_gate: open the live WebView2
window against baseline_commit and confirm what the gate asks for. Say in your
report whether it passed.

Do not commit. Do not push. Do not keep working after the report is written.
Leave the work in the working tree and stop.

When you are done, set phase to ANTIGRAVITY_WORK_READY and ready_for to CLAUDE
in RELAY-STATE.json, and append one history entry describing what you built.

Rule 4 is not a formality: for every test you add, break the code on purpose,
paste the failure, restore, and paste the pass. A mutation that does not break a
test means the test is decoration - say so and replace it. Anchor multi-line
mutations on CRLF; bare \n will silently fail to match in this repo.

If an ambiguous technical fork comes up and the owner is away, do not halt. Send
an informational push via tools/relay_notify.py, take the conservative
non-destructive path that matches the brief, log the decision in your report,
and carry on.
