Nine things that would make this feel professional

Ranked by what they save you, against what they cost to build.

01 · biggest win

Render every format at once

One script, one approval, four aspect ratios. Vertical cuts re-frame around the subject instead of blind-cropping. This alone replaces a whole editing session.

02

Shorts the app proposes

It knows where each beat begins and ends, so it suggests the self-contained 45–60s moments. You tick the ones you like.

03

Series memory

Character bible, voice, style and grade persist across episodes. Episode 7 starts where episode 6 finished — you are making a series, not files.

04

Shot rhythm as one slider

Your segments run 25 seconds. Broadcast cuts every 4–6. One slider splits them into more shots and pulls from the library. The single biggest retention lever.

05

Draft render

Grey placeholders, real narration, sixty seconds. Check pacing before generating a single image.

06

Chapters & description, free

Segment boundaries are already timestamps. Export YouTube chapters, a description and tags with the video.

07

Cost shown before you commit

“This render will generate 3 images · about $0.12.” No paid call happens without that sentence appearing first.

08

Overnight queue

Line up three episodes, walk away. Optional shutdown when the last one lands.

09

Brand kit

Intro, outro, logo bug, lower-third style, music bed — saved once per series and applied on every render.
